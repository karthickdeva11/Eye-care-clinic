08 · Patient FAQ

Before You Visit, Here Are a Few Answers.

Helpful information for a smoother appointment experience.

How do I schedule an eye examination?

You can contact our clinic by phone (9876543210), email (info@stackly.com), or directly through our online appointment form.

Can I request a specific specialist?

Yes! Appointments can be arranged based on individual specialist availability and your visual care needs.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring relevant medical records, previous eye-care reports, and your current glasses or contact lenses when applicable.

How can I reschedule my appointment?

Contact the clinic as early as possible so our team can assist with another available time that works for your schedule.
